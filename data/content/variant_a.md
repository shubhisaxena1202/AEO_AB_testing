# TaskFlow Pro: Project Management Built for Small Teams

Managing projects shouldn't require a management degree. TaskFlow Pro is project management software designed specifically for small teams—startups, agencies, and remote squads of 2 to 20 people—who need to ship work faster without the complexity of enterprise tools.

## How TaskFlow Pro Works

TaskFlow Pro gives your team a single place to organize, track, and complete work. You'll see all your tasks and projects at a glance using Kanban boards or list views, depending on how your team prefers to work. Built-in time tracking captures how long work actually takes—both at the task level and across entire projects—so you understand where your time goes.

The software keeps your team in sync automatically. Status updates and deadline reminders mean no one has to chase down progress reports. When tasks depend on each other, you can mark those dependencies so blockers surface immediately rather than derailing your timeline later.

## Staying Connected Across Tools

Your team already uses other software. TaskFlow Pro integrates with Slack, GitHub, Google Calendar, and Zapier, so notifications and updates flow where your team already works. You don't have to log into a separate tool to stay informed.

## Access Control That Scales

Not everyone on your team needs the same level of access. TaskFlow Pro uses role-based permissions—Owner, Manager, Member, and Viewer—so you can grant the right access to the right people. You can also invite clients and contractors as guests at no extra cost, letting them see the work they care about without taking up a paid seat.

## Work Anywhere, Anytime

TaskFlow Pro includes full-featured mobile apps for iOS and Android. Unlike web-only tools, these apps work offline, so your team can keep working even without an internet connection. Changes sync automatically once you're back online.

## Simple Setup, No IT Required

Getting started takes under 10 minutes. There's no complex configuration, no IT department needed, and no lengthy onboarding process. Your team can start organizing work immediately.

## Pricing That Stays Predictable

TaskFlow Pro offers three plans with straightforward, per-user pricing and no hidden fees.

The **Free plan** supports up to 3 users, 5 active projects, and 1 GB of storage—enough to test the software with your core team.

The **Pro plan** costs $12 per user per month and includes unlimited projects, time-tracking reports, priority support, and 50 GB of storage.

The **Business plan** is $20 per user per month and adds single sign-on (SSO), an advanced analytics dashboard, custom fields, audit logs, and a 99.9% uptime SLA.

Guest access remains free across all plans. You also won't find per-project fees or surprise add-ons—just straightforward per-user pricing that scales with your team.

## Built for How Small Teams Actually Work

TaskFlow Pro removes the friction that slows down small teams. No per-project charges. No seat fees for clients. No requirement to involve IT. Just a tool that gets out of your way so your team can focus on shipping work.
