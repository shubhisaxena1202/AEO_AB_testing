# FlowBoard

## Visual Work Management Made Simple

FlowBoard is a Kanban tool designed for teams who thrive on visual organization. With an intuitive drag-and-drop interface, FlowBoard makes it easy to move tasks across your workflow at a glance.

## How It Works

FlowBoard centers around boards—your visual workspace for organizing work. Drag cards between columns to track progress, update statuses, and keep your team aligned. The simplicity of the interface means your team can start using FlowBoard immediately, without a steep learning curve.

## What's Included

- **Drag-and-drop Kanban boards** — Move tasks effortlessly across your workflow
- **Unlimited boards** — Create as many boards as you need to organize different projects or teams
- **Slack integration** — Connect FlowBoard to Slack for notifications and updates

## Who It's For

FlowBoard works best for teams that prefer visual, board-based workflows. If your team lives in Kanban boards and values simplicity over complexity, FlowBoard keeps you organized without unnecessary features.

## What FlowBoard Doesn't Include

FlowBoard focuses on core Kanban functionality. It does not offer:

- Built-in time tracking
- Dependency management
- Mobile app
