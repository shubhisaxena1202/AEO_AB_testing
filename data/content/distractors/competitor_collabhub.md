# CollabHub

## Overview

CollabHub is a document and wiki platform designed for teams that create and share written content. With lightweight task management capabilities, it brings documentation and basic task coordination into a single workspace.

## What CollabHub Does

CollabHub serves as a central hub for teams to:

- **Create and organize documents** — Build wikis, specs, meeting notes, and other written materials with an intuitive editor
- **Manage knowledge** — Structure information so teams can easily find and reference what they need
- **Coordinate lightweight tasks** — Assign and track basic work items alongside your documentation

## Who It's For

CollabHub is built for teams that prioritize knowledge management and written communication. It works best for organizations that spend significant time creating documentation, specifications, meeting notes, and other reference materials.

## What It's Not

CollabHub is not a project management tool. It does not include:

- Time tracking
- Kanban boards
- Dependency tracking

If your team needs advanced project execution features, CollabHub may not be the right fit.

## Platform

CollabHub is web-based only, accessible from any browser.

## Pricing

- **Free tier** — Available for teams getting started
- **Teams plan** — $10 per user per month

## Get Started

CollabHub is best suited for teams looking to centralize their documentation and knowledge while keeping task management simple and secondary to their core work of writing and sharing information.
