# SwiftTask

## Task Management for Solo Creators

SwiftTask is a task management tool designed specifically for solo freelancers and individuals who need a straightforward way to organize their work and stay on top of deadlines.

## What You Get

**Clean, Minimal Interface**
SwiftTask keeps distractions to a minimum with a streamlined design focused on what matters—your tasks and deadlines.

**Personal Kanban**
Organize your work visually with a personal Kanban board. Move tasks through your workflow at your own pace, with full control over how you structure your process.

**Deadline Reminders**
Never miss an important deadline. SwiftTask keeps you informed so you can focus on delivering quality work.

## Who It's For

SwiftTask is built for solo freelancers and individuals managing their own workload. It's the right fit if you work independently and need a simple, effective way to track tasks and deadlines.

## What It's Not

SwiftTask is not designed for teams. There are no team collaboration features, shared workspaces, or integrations with other tools. If you need to coordinate with others or connect to external applications, SwiftTask isn't the right solution.
