# PlanPro: Project Management Built for Construction and Field Service

PlanPro is a full-featured project management platform designed specifically for construction and field service companies. Whether you're managing multiple job sites, coordinating contractors, or tracking equipment across projects, PlanPro centralizes the tools you need to keep operations running smoothly.

## What PlanPro Includes

- **Scheduling** – Coordinate timelines and resource allocation across projects
- **Budgeting** – Track project costs and maintain financial oversight
- **Equipment Tracking** – Monitor tools, machinery, and assets in the field
- **Contractor Management** – Organize and manage your contractor network

## Who PlanPro Is For

PlanPro is purpose-built for construction companies and field service businesses. If your team works primarily on-site, manages multiple concurrent projects, or relies on contractor coordination, PlanPro's specialized feature set is designed with your workflow in mind.

PlanPro is not designed for software development teams, creative agencies, or other knowledge-work environments where general-purpose project management tools are a better fit.

## Getting Started

PlanPro is priced at $39 per user per month. New customers receive dedicated onboarding to ensure your team can effectively implement the platform and adopt its features.

## Mobile Access

PlanPro includes a mobile app focused on field inspections, allowing your team to capture and document work progress on-site. The mobile experience is optimized for inspection workflows rather than task management.
