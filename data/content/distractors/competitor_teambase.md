# TeamBase: Enterprise Project Management

## Designed for Growing Teams

TeamBase is an enterprise project management platform built for organizations with 50-500 team members. It provides the visibility and control needed to manage complex projects across your entire organization.

## Core Capabilities

**Portfolio Management**
Oversee multiple projects and initiatives across your enterprise with centralized portfolio management tools.

**Resource Planning**
Optimize team allocation and capacity planning to ensure your people are working on the right priorities.

**Organization-Wide Analytics**
Gain insights into project performance, team productivity, and organizational health through comprehensive reporting and analytics.

## Implementation & Setup

TeamBase requires IT setup and onboarding to get your organization up and running. The typical implementation timeline is 2-4 weeks, ensuring your team is properly configured and trained before going live.

## Pricing & Access

- **Starting at $28/user/month** (minimum 25 seats)
- No free tier
- Web-based platform — accessible from any browser
- Desktop-focused experience (no native mobile app)

## Who TeamBase Is For

TeamBase is purpose-built for mid-to-large enterprises that need sophisticated project and resource management. It's ideal for organizations looking to standardize project delivery, improve resource utilization, and gain visibility across their entire portfolio of work.
