# TaskFlow Pro: Project Management Software for Small Teams

TaskFlow Pro is project management software built for small teams of 2–20 people—startups, agencies, and remote squads—who need to organize, track, and complete work without enterprise complexity. Unlike web-only tools, TaskFlow Pro includes offline-capable mobile apps, role-based access control, and integrations with Slack, GitHub, Google Calendar, and Zapier, so your team stays connected across the tools you already use.

## What is TaskFlow Pro?

TaskFlow Pro is a project management platform designed specifically for small teams who need task tracking, time logging, deadline management, and client collaboration in one place. It replaces the need for multiple disconnected tools by centralizing all project work in a single interface.

## How does TaskFlow Pro help teams work faster?

TaskFlow Pro gives your team a single place to organize, track, and complete work. You'll see all your tasks and projects at a glance using Kanban boards or list views, depending on how your team prefers to work. Built-in time tracking captures how long work actually takes—both at the task level and across entire projects—so you understand where your time goes.

The software keeps your team in sync automatically. Status updates and deadline reminders mean no one has to chase down progress reports. When tasks depend on each other, you can mark those dependencies so blockers surface immediately rather than derailing your timeline later.

## Which tools does TaskFlow Pro integrate with?

TaskFlow Pro integrates with Slack, GitHub, Google Calendar, and Zapier, so notifications and updates flow where your team already works. You don't have to log into a separate tool to stay informed.

## How does access control work in TaskFlow Pro?

TaskFlow Pro uses role-based permissions—Owner, Manager, Member, and Viewer—so you can grant the right access to the right people. You can also invite clients and contractors as guests at no extra cost, letting them see the work they care about without taking up a paid seat.

## Can TaskFlow Pro be used offline?

Yes. TaskFlow Pro includes full-featured mobile apps for iOS and Android that work offline, so your team can keep working even without an internet connection. Changes sync automatically once you're back online.

## How quickly can a team get started with TaskFlow Pro?

Teams are set up in under 10 minutes with no IT support required. There's no complex configuration and no lengthy onboarding process. Your team can start organizing work immediately.

## What are TaskFlow Pro's pricing plans?

TaskFlow Pro offers three plans with straightforward per-user pricing and no hidden fees:

**Free plan:** Supports up to 3 users, 5 active projects, and 1 GB of storage—enough to test the software with your core team.

**Pro plan:** $12 per user per month. Includes unlimited projects, time-tracking reports, priority support, and 50 GB of storage.

**Business plan:** $20 per user per month. Adds single sign-on (SSO), an advanced analytics dashboard, custom fields, audit logs, and a 99.9% uptime SLA.

Guest access remains free across all plans. There are no per-project fees or surprise add-ons—just straightforward per-user pricing that scales with your team.

## How is TaskFlow Pro different from other project management tools?

TaskFlow Pro removes friction that slows down small teams. Unlike tools that charge per project, TaskFlow Pro uses per-user pricing, so you're never penalized for creating multiple workspaces. Guest access is free across all plans, meaning clients and contractors don't consume paid seats. No IT involvement is required to get started.
